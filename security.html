Be Safe · Be Smart · Stay Protected

🛡 Security First

Digital safety is about protecting accounts, privacy and personal data. Never share passwords or verification codes.

🔐 Quick Safety Guide

Account Security — Use strong, unique passwords and 2FA.
Telegram Safety — Review active sessions and protect login codes.
Phishing Awareness — Verify suspicious links before opening them.
Privacy — Avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly.
Downloads — Be careful with unknown files and apps.
Updates — Keep your device and important apps updated.
⚠️ This page is for general digital-safety awareness, not a promise of complete security.
